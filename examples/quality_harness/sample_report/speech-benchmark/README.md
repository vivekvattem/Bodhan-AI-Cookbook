# Speech runtime and coverage diagnostic

This is a previously inspected development workload, not a held-out accuracy test. It contains 200 rows in Hindi, Tamil and Bengali: the 120 demo rows plus 80 related punctuation-modified repeats. The workload has eight underlying English source groups. The run uses warm caches and disables judge dispatch, so it does not establish the assignment's cold, complete 200-item end-to-end runtime target.

The original run completed in 2,187.045 seconds (36 minutes 27 seconds). Synthesis and audio signal checks evaluated 200/200 items. Transcription evaluated 32/200; 167 checks were unavailable after rate limiting and one audio clip exceeded the 30-second transcription limit. Alignment was inconclusive for the 32 transcribed items and unavailable for the other 168. The 200 measured clips totaled 2,733.909 seconds, with durations from 5.888 to 30.549 seconds.

A separately capped retry used saved audio for the 167 rate-limited items. It completed 16 transcriptions, then the endpoint paused after three more HTTP 429 attempts. A later single retry used saved audio for the remaining 151 items, completed 16 transcriptions and paused again after three HTTP 429 attempts. Combined coverage was 64/200 transcribed, 64 inconclusive alignments, and 136 unavailable (135 rate-limited and one over 30 seconds). The retries took 50.297 and 49.139 seconds; synthesis was skipped because the WAVs were supplied. There was no added pacing between successful calls in the second retry; the existing serial execution and bounded backoff applied.

The original run made 235 Bodhan attempts, with 222 successful; estimated new known spend was ₹12.53244 and ₹0.4696 was held for 13 failed-attempt charges that remain unknown. Its 210 cache hits comprised 74 synthesis responses, 120 back-translations and 16 transcriptions. The first retry made 19 attempts (16 successful), with 167 cached back-translations, ₹0.34 estimated new known spend, and ₹0.06 held for three failed attempts. The second made 19 attempts (16 successful), with 151 cached back-translations, ₹0.396667 estimated new known spend, and ₹0.085 held for three failed attempts. Combined estimated new known spend was ₹13.269107 with ₹0.6146 uncertain exposure; reservations are not invoices. Judge dispatch was disabled in all runs and had no new cost.

The original and retries are separate reports and invocations; retries did not rewrite the original report. All three replayed byte-identically with networking blocked and zero provider attempts. No threshold was tuned from these inspected data. Generated audio and caches are not in Git.
